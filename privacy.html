PRIVACY

Lethometry is a research archive. It has no accounts, no comments, and no advertising. Here is exactly what is collected when you read it.

LAST UPDATED 2026-10-24

WHAT IS COLLECTED

One thing: anonymous visit counts, so the archive knows which pages are read and which are ignored.

DataWhyWho receives it
Page URL, referrer, and approximate regionTo count visits and find pages that need workGoogle Analytics 4
Device and browser typeTo know whether the archive should be reworked for a specific deviceGoogle Analytics 4
IP addressTruncated and anonymised before it is recordedNot retained

Nothing else. There is no account system, no profile, no advertising identifier, no cross-site tracking, and no third-party tracker of any kind. No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.

COOKIES

Google Analytics 4 sets a first-party cookie named _ga in your browser. It is used only to distinguish one visit from the next and to avoid counting the same page view twice. There is no advertising cookie, and no cookie is shared with any other site.

OPTING OUT

You can decline analytics entirely and read every article. Lethometry works identically for you either way — nothing is gated, no content is hidden, and no feature is withheld.

To opt out, use the Google Analytics browser extension for your browser, or install a content blocker such as uBlock Origin. Both stop the analytics request before it leaves your device. Every article remains fully readable either way.

NOTE: NO SERVER-SIDE PERSONALISATION EXISTS ON THIS SITE, SO BLOCKING ANALYTICS DOES NOT REDUCE WHAT YOU CAN READ.

HOSTING AND LOGS

The site is served as static files through Vercel. The hosting provider keeps short-lived request logs for security and abuse prevention. Those logs are not combined with the analytics described above and are not used for profiling.
